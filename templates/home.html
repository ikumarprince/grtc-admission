ABOUT OUR INSTITUTION

Empowering Youth Through Quality Skill Training

Gyanoday Rojgar Training Centre (GRTC) is dedicated to bridging the gap between education and employment. Located at Khaspur, Ramchandra Pahalwan Path near Bajaj Showroom, Patna, our center provides job-oriented vocational and technical training.

Our state-of-the-art practical laboratories, experienced instructors, and structured curriculum empower trainees with hands-on confidence in Computer IT, Hotel Management & F&B, and General Duty Assistance (GDA).
